# Supplementary data

`GBE_Supplementary_Data_Tables.xlsx` contains Supplementary Data Tables S1-S20
and a contents sheet. The workbook provides compact sequence, isoform,
methylation, expression, chromatin, and model summaries cited by the
manuscript.

`MANIFEST.tsv` lists the eight larger row-level datasets, their record counts,
descriptions, source-result paths, and SHA-256 checksums. Those datasets,
together with the sequence alignments, phylogenetic tree, and promoter-sequence
sets, have been prepared for a companion Zenodo deposit rather than duplicated
in GitHub. `REPRODUCIBILITY_FILES.tsv` identifies the staged alignments, tree,
and sequence sets, including record counts and SHA-256 checksums. The DOI will
be added before publication.

Blank workbook cells denote unavailable or inapplicable values. Numerical
values are retained at the precision of the validated analysis outputs.
